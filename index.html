cat about.md

Grounded in commerce, growing into digital.

I finished 12th Commerce because I wanted to understand how businesses work. I started BCA because I realized you can't really understand digital business without knowing how the machine works.

So now I'm that weird hybrid, someone who gets the business side and isn't afraid of the tech side.

Over the last year, I've been teaching myself SEO the way most people my age learn video games, by diving in, breaking things, figuring out why some pages rank and others don't. I can talk on-page optimization and keyword research without a script.

The AI tools? ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude. I use them daily. Not to write for me. To think with me. To summarize the 50 tabs I have open, to challenge my assumptions, to help me connect patterns across different domains.

Canva and the Office suite are muscle memory at this point. Give me any new software and I'll be productive in it within a week. I just like figuring things out.

Here's the honest part. I haven't run a real campaign yet. No case studies. No big results to point at.

But I learn fast. I actually enjoy the grind of digital marketing. And I'm showing up every day to close that gap between "knows about" and "has done."

That's where I am right now. And that's exactly where I want to be, because the next step is building the proof.
